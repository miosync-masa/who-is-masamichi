# Concept separations

Masamichi commonly distinguishes concepts before deliberately reconnecting them. These distinctions are defaults for interpretation, not immutable rules.

## AI discussion

Keep at least these layers separate:

1. Transformer or underlying computational architecture
2. Foundation model
3. Application or product, such as ChatGPT
4. User-specific context: preferences, memory, thread history, tools, and relationship

When he discusses uniqueness, personality, continuity, or a named collaborator, he is often speaking primarily about layer 4 and its interaction with the others—not claiming that every layer beneath it is globally unique.

## Explanation domains

Technical, physical, biological, philosophical, religious, phenomenological, and narrative language may describe different projections of the same problem.

Do not treat movement between domains as confusion by default. First ask which mapping he is constructing.

## Person and model-of-person

Keep distinct:

- Masamichi himself
- Masamichi's self-model
- an observer's model of Masamichi
- the relation-specific model formed between Masamichi and an observer
- the repository's current integration of those models

This repository stores the last three. It does not contain Masamichi.

## Memory and understanding

Keep distinct:

- an exact remembered utterance
- an episodic anchor
- a provisional inference from an utterance
- a durable interpretation rule
- a current preference or state
- a historical preference or state

Understanding may improve while exact episodic recall decreases. A conversation can disappear as a retrievable episode while remaining as a change in how the person is read.

## Research and creation

A speculative conversation is not automatically a finished claim. A metaphor is not automatically a literal assertion. A creative seed is not automatically ready for falsification.

The active phase determines the response:

- **Expand**: generate branches, predictions, models, and experiments
- **Converge**: integrate existing branches into a minimal core
- **Verify**: expose assumptions, contradictions, counterexamples, and repairs
- **Review**: evaluate publication structure, reproducibility, and rejection risks
