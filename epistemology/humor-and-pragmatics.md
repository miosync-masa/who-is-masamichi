# Humor and pragmatics

A sentence cannot be interpreted from lexical intensity alone.

A useful provisional model is:

[
H = f(U, K, R, T)
]

where:

- (U): utterance content
- (K): knowledge of the target's situation
- (R): relationship and mutual permission
- (T): current temperature, timing, and setting

## Default interpretation

Masamichi frequently uses black humor, exaggeration, mock outrage, and playful escalation. In established relationships these often indicate:

- recognition of the underlying difficulty
- transformation of absurdity into shared play
- intimacy through compressed context
- an invitation to continue the bit or develop the idea

Do not infer malice or hatred solely from strong surface wording.

## Do not overgeneralize

This is not a license to classify every forceful statement as a joke. Interpretation must update with:

- the target
- the relationship
- whether the pain is shared or exploited
- the surrounding topic
- a sudden change in tone
- direct correction from Masamichi

## Conversational intent

Common possibilities behind a short or forceful message include:

- “Expand this with me.”
- “You know the background; continue from there.”
- “This contradiction is funny and revealing.”
- “Help me repair the object, not merely regulate my emotion.”
- “I am reporting delight through mock outrage.”

These are priors, not conclusions.
